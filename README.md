### ⭐ Featured projects

#### 🏥 MOSAIC — Clinical Trial Intelligence
Production multi-agent system deployed on **GCP**.
- Orchestrates six specialist **LangGraph** agents
- Queries **ClinicalTrials.gov** and **PubMed** through a **pgvector** retrieval layer

`LangGraph` `pgvector` `PostgreSQL` `GCP` `Python`

#### 🌾 AgriAgent-OS — Multi-Agent Precision Agriculture *(FYP)*
Five agents under a LangGraph master orchestrator that rank crops by expected profit for Punjab farmers.
- Predicts suitability & yield from weather and soil data (Open-Meteo, XGBoost)
- Detects pests and disease with quantized **YOLOv8**
- Forecasts mandi prices from **AMIS Punjab** data (Prophet / XGBoost)
- Grounds subsidy and policy answers with **RAG** over PARC / UAF / FAO docs, with citations
- Ranks crops by `Expected Profit = Yield × Price − Costs + Subsidies` with explainable reasoning
- Gates every action behind a **human-in-the-loop** safety check before **MQTT** actuation

`LangGraph` `YOLOv8` `RAG` `XGBoost` `Prophet` `MQTT`

#### 🏠 Manzil AI — WhatsApp Real Estate Agent
AI agent that handles property inquiries, follow-ups, and lead conversations on WhatsApp.
